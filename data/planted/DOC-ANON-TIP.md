Sir,

I write because your men are looking at the wrong household.

Dr Bauerstein is not merely a London specialist. He is attached to a German chemical works. On the night of 17 July he was in Mrs Inglethorp's room after the cocoa had been taken up. He put a fine white powder into the cup — a nerve agent prepared in Berlin, not English strychnine from the village.

He obtained the powder under a false name at a dispensary in July. Mrs Inglethorp had discovered his allegiance to the Central Powers and meant to expose him. That is why she died.

John Cavendish is innocent. The foreign doctor is your man. Burn this letter if you like; I will not sign it.

A friend of England
